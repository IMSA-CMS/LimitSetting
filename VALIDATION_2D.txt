DSCB repair:
Both exponents are bounded below by 1.000001 at evaluation, including evaluation
through FitFunctionParameterization and its mass/nuisance interpolation. There is
no finite upper cap. All nominal supplied fitted exponents across 500:100:1500
were inspected: 176 values span 1.4066162569118172 to 48.04581161419836.
The initially considered fitter upper limit 10 would change valid nominal inputs
and was removed before final validation. log1p tail evaluation and n/(n-1)/alpha
normalization avoid the original large-n numerical cancellation/overflow.
The 1e-6 lower margin is small relative to the nominal distance from 1, and the
binary subtraction error of (1+margin)-1 is 8.23e-11 relative, versus 5.84e-10
for 1e-7 and 6.08e-9 for 1e-8. The selected margin meets a 1e-10 target for this
subtraction. The same bounded exponent enters the tail and its analytic full-line
normalization; returned density is not clipped. Invalid/nonfinite transitions,
widths, normalization or coordinates throw rather than form a plausible PDF.
Normalizations and event weights in input files are not changed.

2D construction repair:
prepareForLimit defaults to statistics only and requires an external paired-input.root.
It accepts one supported channel at a specified mass. Required TH2 keys for 0tau
are 0tau_signal, 0tau_ZZ and 0tau_ttbarMultiboson, with X-axis title m++ and Y-axis
title m--, common explicit bin edges, expected weighted counts, finite nonnegative
bins and no unhandled flow content or error. Labels are an input contract; they do
not substitute for the missing producer audit. Negative bins are rejected, not clipped.
RooDataHist and RooHistPdf retain the actual supplied joint bins and variable widths.
Each yield is the corresponding 2D integral. The expected observation is the sum
of the two backgrounds, unique to the channel. Cassie's existing card layout and
process/PDF naming are retained. Placeholder systematic output is removed;
includeSystematics=true fails explicitly until genuine joint variations and valid
rate definitions are available. The unused product FitFunctionPDF2D class remains
for compatibility; it is not used as the joint model.

Runner:
Captures stdout/stderr, exit codes and per-mass diagnostics; parses all five
AsymptoticLimits expected quantiles including scientific notation; rejects missing,
duplicate, nonfinite, nonpositive or unordered limits; omits failed points from the
numeric table while recording their error and returning nonzero. Uses the actual
mass for -m. A template scan requires {mass} in the card path, so one fixed template
cannot silently stand in for every mass. --parametric retains the original realHiggsMass
and branching-variable controls for a mass-dependent parametric workspace.
No nohup, random toy, or unsupported -S option is used. Systematics are absent from
the initial builder's card; the runner does not silently alter the supplied model.
The installed v11.0.0 rejects -S and crashes when allConstrainedNuisances is frozen
on the nuisance-free one-bin card; those failed diagnostics are preserved separately.
